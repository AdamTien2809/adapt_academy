🎯 Training Focus: Moving from Knowing → Applying → Analysing → Evaluating

3 Core Learning Objectives & Rationale

Targeting operational agility, cross-functional awareness, and integrated trade-off mastery.

P3 • APPLY
⚡
Learning Objective

Apply adaptive communication, open-mindedness, and problem-solving when responding to unfamiliar or changing operational conditions.

Rationale

Learners are expected to move beyond understanding concepts and apply them when operational conditions become unfamiliar, uncertain, or change unexpectedly.

P4 • ANALYSE
🌐
Learning Objective

Analyse stakeholders, cross-functional impacts, and potential organisational consequences before responding to an operational decision.

Rationale

Learners need to move beyond analysing an issue only from their own functional perspective. They should be able to analyse stakeholder interests, cross-functional impacts, and wider organisational consequences before making or recommending an operational decision.

P5 • EVALUATE
⚖️
Learning Objective

Evaluate alternative responses and justify a solution that balances operational requirements, cross-functional impacts, and wider organisational outcomes.

Rationale

P5 requires learners to go beyond selecting an answer. They must compare alternatives, consider trade-offs, evaluate consequences, and justify an appropriate response based on operational and organisational considerations.

? Why are P1, P2 and P6 excluded?
View exclusions rationale
P1 – Remember Not Required

Already achieved a 100% benchmark through regular competency assessments.

P2 – Understand Not Required

Learners already understand the organisation’s required standards, procedures, and expected behaviours.

P6 – Create Not Required

The training focuses on resolving operational situations within established safety and procedural boundaries, rather than creating new procedures outside controlled processes.

PROGRAM SYNTHESIS

ASSESS

Explore different options and consider alternative approaches when the standard procedure is no longer enough.
